MEDIC-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 3: Support)
========================================================

Medic-Bot, a friendly field medic robot balancing on a single ball wheel, with idle and
roll animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/medic_idle.png, medic_roll.png       rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                         same with ground shadow
  sheets/medic_shadow.png                     shadow alone
  sheets/medic.json                           frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                 shared series palette

The full Robot Series Pack 3: Support adds:
  - Medic-Bot heal, hit and death animations
  - Cargo-Bot (robot forklift) and Welder-Bot (four-legged welder), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
