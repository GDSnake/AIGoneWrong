TORSO-CRAWLER — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 12: Horror)
============================================================

Torso-Crawler, half a robot endoskeleton that drags itself along on its arms (skull head with the
pink-red series eye and teeth, open ribcage with a pulsing pink heart, spine and torn cables trailing
on the floor), with idle and crawl animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/torso_idle.png, torso_crawl.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                      same with ground shadow
  sheets/torso_shadow.png                  shadow alone
  sheets/torso.json                        frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                              palette

The crawl is timed for 2 px across / 1 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at that
speed and the hands don't slide.

The full Robot Series Pack 12: Horror adds:
  - Torso-Crawler lunge, hit and death animations
  - Seeker-Squid and Slender-Bot, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
