PTERO-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 11: Dino-Mechs)
============================================================

Ptero-Bot, a pteranodon robot enemy (gunmetal membrane wings with orange hazard chevrons, pick-axe
head with a swept orange crest, pink-red cyclops eye, big talons, brass thrusters), with idle and fly
animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/ptero_idle.png, ptero_fly.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (96x96 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                    same with ground shadow
  sheets/ptero_shadow.png                shadow alone
  sheets/ptero.json                      frame counts, timings, pivot (48,79)
  palette.hex                            palette

The fly cycle is timed for 3 px across / 1.5 px down per frame on diagonals. Ptero-Bot hovers:
keep the pivot (and the shadow) on the ground.

The full Robot Series Pack 11: Dino-Mechs adds:
  - Ptero-Bot dive, hit and death animations
  - Rex-Bot and Trike-Bot, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
