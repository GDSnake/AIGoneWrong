HOVER-BIKE — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 14: War Vehicles)
===============================================================

Hover-Bike, a riderless enemy raider bike floating on hover skids (gunmetal body with orange hazard bands,
pink-red series eye as a headlamp, twin blasters, flared brass engine nozzle), with idle and fly
animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/hoverbike_idle.png, hoverbike_fly.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)

  sheets/hoverbike.json                          frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                    palette

"fly" loops; move the sprite 2 px across and 1 px down per frame on the diagonals.
