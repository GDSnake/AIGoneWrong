BRAWLER-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 16: Heroes II)
=============================================================

Brawler-Bot, a playable hero robot boxer (rust orange plating, orange helmet, teal punk crest, cyan
series eye, two huge gauntlets held up in a boxing guard), with idle and run animations in all 8
isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/brawler_idle.png, brawler_run.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                        same with ground shadow
  sheets/brawler_shadow.png                  shadow alone
  sheets/brawler.json                        frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                palette

"run" loops; move the sprite 3 px across and 1.5 px down per frame on the diagonals.

The full Robot Series Pack 16: Heroes II adds:
  - Brawler-Bot punch, slam (ground pound with shockwave), dash, hit and death animations
  - Ranger-Bot (jetpack gunslinger) and Mage-Bot (floating tech-mage), 7 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
