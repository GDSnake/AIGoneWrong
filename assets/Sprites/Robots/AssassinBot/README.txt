ASSASSIN-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 7: Specialists)
===============================================================

Assassin-Bot, a monowheel robot with a cyan LED rim, a ninja bandana and twin blades, with
idle and roll animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/assassin_idle.png, assassin_roll.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                           same with ground shadow
  sheets/assassin_shadow.png                    shadow alone
  sheets/assassin.json                          frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                   shared series palette

The roll is timed for 2 px across / 1 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at that
speed and the wheel spins at ground speed.

The full Robot Series Pack 7: Specialists adds:
  - Assassin-Bot slash, hit and death animations
  - Destroyer-Bot and Engineer-Bot, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
