SCORPION-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 6: Bosses)
==========================================================

Scorpion-Bot, an armoured scorpion boss, with idle and walk animations in all 8 isometric
directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE). 128x128 cells at the same scale as the 64x64
robots of the Robot Series.

  sheets/scorpion_idle.png, scorpion_walk.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (128x128 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                           same with ground shadow
  sheets/scorpion_shadow.png                    shadow alone
  sheets/scorpion.json                          frame counts, timings, pivot (64,96)
  palette.hex                                   shared series palette

The walk is timed for 2 px across / 1 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at that
speed and the feet never slide.

The full Robot Series Pack 6: Bosses adds:
  - Scorpion-Bot sting (laser), hit and death animations
  - Spider-Tank and Hive-Ship, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
