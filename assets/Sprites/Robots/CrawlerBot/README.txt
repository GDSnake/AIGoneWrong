CRAWLER-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 2: Hostiles)
===========================================================

Crawler-Bot, a six-legged spider robot enemy, with idle and walk animations
in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/crawler_idle.png, crawler_walk.png      rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                            same with ground shadow
  sheets/crawler_shadow.png                      shadow alone
  sheets/crawler.json                            frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                    shared series palette

The full Robot Series Pack 2: Hostiles adds:
  - Crawler-Bot bite, hit and death animations
  - Sentry-Bot (tripod gun turret) and Stalker-Bot (reverse-knee walker), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
