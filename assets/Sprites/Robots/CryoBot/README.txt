CRYO-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 15: Elementals)
===========================================================

Cryo-Bot, a hovering enemy frost core (gunmetal shell opened around a glowing ice core, orange hazard
bands, pink-red series eye, three orbiting ice crystals), with idle and drift animations in all 8
isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/cryo_idle.png, cryo_drift.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                    same with ground shadow
  sheets/cryo_shadow.png                 shadow alone
  sheets/cryo.json                       frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                            palette

"drift" loops; move the sprite 2 px across and 1 px down per frame on the diagonals.

The full Robot Series Pack 15: Elementals adds:
  - Cryo-Bot frost (ice spikes erupting from the ground), hit and death animations
  - Pyro-Bot (fire) and Volt-Bot (lightning), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
