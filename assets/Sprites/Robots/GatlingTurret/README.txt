Gatling-Turret, an armoured enemy turret with twin rotary guns (gunmetal bunker with orange hazard
bands, pink-red series eye, five-barrel chrome gatlings, tracer rounds and flying casings), with
idle and fire animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).
