TROOPER-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 17: Grunts)
==========================================================

Trooper-Bot, a small enemy rifle infantry robot (gunmetal armour, orange hazard stripes, domed helmet,
pink-red series eye, backpack radio with a pink antenna beacon, rifle at port arms), with idle and walk
animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/trooper_idle.png, trooper_walk.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                         same with ground shadow
  sheets/trooper_shadow.png                   shadow alone
  sheets/trooper.json                         frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                 palette

"walk" loops; move the sprite 2 px across and 1 px down per frame on the diagonals.

The full Robot Series Pack 17: Grunts adds:
  - Trooper-Bot fire (3-round burst), hit and death animations
  - Hopper-Bot (robot flea) and Wasp-Drone (flying robot wasp), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
