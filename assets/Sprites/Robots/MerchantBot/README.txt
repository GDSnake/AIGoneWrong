MERCHANT-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 18: Town NPCs)
==============================================================

Merchant-Bot, a friendly travelling trader robot (rust orange body, cream head, cyan series eye, teal
neckerchief, a huge backpack stall with crates, a striped awning, a lantern and a pot), with idle and walk
animations in all 8 isometric directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/merchant_idle.png, merchant_walk.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                           same with ground shadow
  sheets/merchant_shadow.png                    shadow alone
  sheets/merchant.json                          frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                                   palette

"walk" loops; move the sprite 2 px across and 1 px down per frame on the diagonals.

The full Robot Series Pack 18: Town NPCs adds:
  - Merchant-Bot talk, sell (offers a potion) and greet animations
  - Elder-Bot (quest-giver) and Barkeep-Bot (tavern keeper), 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
