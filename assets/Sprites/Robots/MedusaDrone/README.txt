MEDUSA-DRONE — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 9: Bio-Mechs)
==============================================================

Medusa-Drone, a floating jellyfish robot enemy (gunmetal bell, orange hazard band, glowing core,
cable tentacles, pink-red cyclops eye), with idle and drift animations in all 8 isometric
directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/jelly_idle.png, jelly_drift.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (96x96 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                      same with ground shadow
  sheets/jelly_shadow.png                  shadow alone
  sheets/jelly.json                        frame counts, timings, pivot (48,79)
  palette.hex                              palette

The drift is timed for 3 px across / 1.5 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at
that speed and it glides without sliding.

The full Robot Series Pack 9: Bio-Mechs adds:
  - Medusa-Drone shock, hit and death animations
  - Mantis-Bot and Rhino-Beetle-Bot, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
