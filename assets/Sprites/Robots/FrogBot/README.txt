FROG-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 5: Pets)
====================================================

Frog-Bot, a chibi robot frog pet, with idle and hop animations in all 8 isometric
directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/frog_idle.png, frog_hop.png       rows = directions, cols = frames (64x64 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                      same with ground shadow
  sheets/frog_shadow.png                   shadow alone
  sheets/frog.json                         frame counts, timings, pivot (32,53)
  palette.hex                              shared series palette

The hop is timed for 2 px across / 1 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at that
speed and the feet never slide.

The full Robot Series Pack 5: Pets adds:
  - Frog-Bot croak, hit and death animations
  - Dog-Bot and Cat-Bot, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
