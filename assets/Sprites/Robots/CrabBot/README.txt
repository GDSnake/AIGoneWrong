CRAB-BOT — FREE SAMPLE  (Robot Series, Pack 10: Abyss)
======================================================

Crab-Bot, an armoured crab robot enemy (gunmetal shell with an orange hazard rim, pink-red eye on
a stalk, two chrome pincers, six arched legs), with idle and walk animations in all 8 isometric
directions (S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE).

  sheets/crab_idle.png, crab_walk.png   rows = directions, cols = frames (96x96 cells)
  sheets/*_shadow.png                   same with ground shadow
  sheets/crab_shadow.png                shadow alone
  sheets/crab.json                      frame counts, timings, pivot (48,79)
  palette.hex                           palette

The walk is timed for 3 px across / 1.5 px down per frame on diagonals: move the sprite at that
speed and the feet don't slide.

The full Robot Series Pack 10: Abyss adds:
  - Crab-Bot pinch, hit and death animations
  - Octo-Bot and Shark-Sub, 5 animations each
  - .aseprite source files with tags, single-frame PNGs, GIF previews
